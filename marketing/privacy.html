Privacy Policy for SAM Tracker

Effective Date: August 11, 2025

Thank you for your interest in SAM Tracker. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and sign up for our waitlist.

1. What Information Do We Collect?

Email Address: We collect your email address to notify you about early access, product updates, and official launch information related to SAM Tracker.

Analytics Data (Non-Personal): Like most websites, we use privacy‑friendly analytics services (such as Plausible or Fathom) to understand our website traffic. This helps us see how many visitors we get and which pages are popular. This data is aggregated and anonymized; we do not collect your IP address or use cookies to track you across other websites.

2. How Do We Use Your Information?

We use the information we collect for one purpose only: to communicate with you directly about SAM Tracker. This includes sending you an invitation for early access, sharing important updates about our product development, and notifying you when we officially launch.

3. Will We Share Your Information?

No. We will never sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share your email address with any third party for marketing purposes. Your information is for our internal use only to manage the waitlist and communicate with you about our product.

4. How Do We Protect Your Information?

We take the security of your information seriously. We use standard technical and administrative security measures to protect your data from loss, misuse, and unauthorized access.

5. How Long Do We Keep Your Information?

We will retain your email address for as long as SAM Tracker is in development and active. If you wish to be removed from our waitlist at any time, you can unsubscribe directly from any email you receive from us or by contacting us.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be indicated by a new “Effective Date” at the top of this page.
